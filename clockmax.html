Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you install.

Is Clock Max free?

Yes. Clock Max is completely free, with no account, subscription, or in-app purchase required.

What reminder schedules are supported?

One-time, daily, weekly, and custom weekday schedules. Every alarm can be set to any time of day, precise to the minute.

Which browsers does Clock Max support?

Clock Max is published on the Chrome Web Store and runs in Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers, including Microsoft Edge and Brave.

Do I need an account to use Clock Max?

No account is needed. Install the extension and start setting reminders right away.

Does Clock Max work offline?

Yes. Alarms, timers, the world clock, and your to-do list all work offline because everything runs locally in your browser.

Where is my data stored?

All alarms, timers, tasks, and settings are stored locally on your device using the browser's storage API. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.
