Privacy Policy for ColorPick Pro

Last Updated: April 6, 2026

Introduction

ColorPick Pro ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Chrome extension.

Information We Collect

ColorPick Pro does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local).

Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored locally on your device to provide the extension's functionality:

  • Saved colors - Colors you have picked and saved for future reference
  • User preferences - Language settings, theme mode (light/dark), default copy format
  • Keyboard shortcuts - Your configured keyboard shortcut preferences
  • Rating status - Whether you have rated the extension (to avoid repeated prompts)

How We Use Your Data

All stored data is used solely to:

  • Provide core extension functionality (color picking, saving colors)
  • Remember your preferences across browser sessions
  • Enable keyboard shortcuts for quick color picking

Data Sharing

We do NOT share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties. All data remains on your local device.

Third-Party Services

ColorPick Pro does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Permissions Used

The extension requests the following permissions for functional purposes only:

  • storage - Required to save your saved colors and settings locally
  • activeTab - Required to access the current tab for color picking
  • commands - Required to register keyboard shortcuts for quick color picking
  • scripting - Required to inject the color picker functionality into web pages
  • windows - Required for managing extension windows
  • tabs - Required to communicate between the extension and web pages
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) - Required to pick colors from any website you visit

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, data security is maintained by your browser's built-in security mechanisms. We do not have access to your data.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can clear all saved colors and settings within the extension
  • You can clear all data by uninstalling the extension
  • You can modify or delete individual saved colors within the extension

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
